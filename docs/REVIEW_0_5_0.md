# Developer and consumer review — v0.5.0

This is a subjective review of the working build and actual renders, not a measured market score or a claim of commercial readiness.

The game now has a coherent playable journey: the drawings travel from the desk through blocks, paper forest, arcade and signal city into the final core, then restore the colors in the ending. The new scenes create a noticeable change in atmosphere. Distinct warnings and a single active hazard zone keep the additional danger readable. The strongest visual ingredients remain the painted worlds, hollow silhouettes, huge colored weapons and compact ink HUD.

Rally has moved from a small detour toward a complete second activity. Players can master a bend, collect fuel, hop an obstacle, change cars, race together and save a course they drew themselves. Persistent creations and personal bests provide more reason to return than simply repeating a race.

The integrated review found and fixed invisible menu text, scenery drawing over result panels, a warning graphic covering its own label, incorrect result-button focus order, valid racing saves being misread as damaged, and optional hazards suppressing Dark lord’s signature rift. The closing movie’s floor alignment and actor overlap were also corrected after viewing actual frames.

From a senior developer perspective this is approximately **7.5/10 as a family prototype**: the main loops work, components are reusable, and automated coverage is broad. That rating does not establish how much children will enjoy it. A consumer score would require observed family play: whether the controls make sense without coaching, whether the final bosses feel fair, whether the editor inspires creations, and whether players voluntarily return.

The largest remaining gaps to a commercial release are richer character acting, smoother cinematic transitions, wider long-term progression, physical controller/hardware checks, and difficulty tuning based on real players. The ending supplies closure but remains a brief captioned 2D story. The existing varied environments and gameplay are worth testing before adding more content.
