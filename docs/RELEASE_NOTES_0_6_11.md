# Doodle Rumble v0.6.11

This update makes Hard feel more active when the computer is playing one of the six regular color fighters. They move sooner after a hit, approach more decisively, and choose their special attacks more often.

In a fixed 45-second controller comparison, Hard made 47 attack decisions, up from 36 in v0.6.10. The test also confirms that the fighter starts a new readable attack tell about 0.05 seconds after hit-stun ends. The regular hit-stun and temporary protection remain in place, so opponents are still allowed a clear, fair recovery window.

Easy and Medium, boss behavior, hazards, damage rules and Doodle Rally are unchanged.
