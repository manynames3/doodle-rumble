# Doodle Rumble v0.7.7 — Doodle Workshop photo cleanup

This update makes it easier to bring a real paper doodle into the Workshop and color a starter fighter the way you want.

## What changed

- **Warm-gray page removal:** the photo cleanup control now reaches the gray-beige paper common in shadowed or compressed photos. The supplied yellow-marker drawing clears at the default setting while the marker strokes remain visible.
- **Visible cleanup preview:** the cleaned transparent image updates beside the original as you adjust the slider; the original remains visible for positioning page corners. Keep and Erase corrections apply in the order drawn.
- **Separate border control:** page cleanup and the **White edge** sticker outline are distinct. Keep and Erase brushes remain available for pale marks that need a manual touch-up.
- **Clearer photo rigging:** the importer keeps showing the full photo until all six body cutouts are traced and valid. Small slivers are rejected, and Save & Fight directs you to the next part to repair. Limb tracing remains guided/manual; there is no automatic pose recognition.
- **Two drawing colors:** **Fighter** recolors the starter body and **Pen** colors new strokes independently. Both are saved, undoable and compatible with older doodles.
